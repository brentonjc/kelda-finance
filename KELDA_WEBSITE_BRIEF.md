# Kelda Finance — Marketing Website Brief

## Brand Overview

**Product Name:** Kelda Finance  
**Tagline:** *An Australian personal finance app made for singles, couples and families — runs entirely in your browser, installs on your phone like a native app.*

**Brand Promise:** Total financial clarity and control — Australian-focused PFM that's deeply personal, completely private, and genuinely helpful.

---

## Product Positioning

### Who We Are
Kelda Finance is a next-generation personal finance management app built specifically for Australian households. Unlike traditional banking apps or spreadsheet-based systems, Kelda brings clarity to your complete financial picture in one place — from bank accounts and mortgages to superannuation, investments, and personal goals.

### Why Kelda?
- **Australian-First**: Currency in AUD, loan structures, tax tracking, superannuation calculations, and household naming conventions (Offset accounts, home value, Council Rates) — all built for Australian users
- **Truly Private**: All data lives in your browser; nothing leaves your device. No servers, no accounts, no external tracking
- **Works Everywhere**: Runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) on desktop, tablet, and phone. Install on home screen for a native app experience
- **For Everyone**: Single professionals, couples, and families — with multi-profile support and household data sharing
- **Zero Cost**: Free to use forever. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium tiers

## Target Audience

### Primary (who we're building for first)
1. **Financially Savvy Couples & Families (25–55 years)**
   - Homeowners with mortgages
   - Multiple income streams
   - Active savers and investors
   - Want a holistic view of family wealth
   - Privacy-conscious; distrust cloud-based financial tools

2. **Self-Employed & Freelancers**
   - Track personal income vs. business income
   - Need tax planning insights
   - Want complete financial control
   - Frustrated with banking apps lacking detail

3. **Superannuation Planners (40–65 years)**
   - Track super across multiple funds
   - Model retirement scenarios
   - Want to understand wealth composition
   - Need tax-effective planning insights

### Secondary
- Tech-forward individuals who value privacy and control
- Australians concerned about data sovereignty
- People with complex finances (property investors, multiple businesses, significant assets)

## Key Features to Highlight

### 1. **Dashboard — Your Financial Picture at a Glance**
- Net Worth Tracker with historical trend line
- Health Score (0–100) showing financial wellbeing
- Income, Expenses & Savings KPIs
- Habit Streaks (savings momentum tracking)
- Smart Insights with actionable nudges

### 2. **Transactions — Categorise Earnings & Spending**
- Add / edit / delete with merchant names
- Auto-categorisation rules
- Bulk recategorise multiple rows
- Full history searchable and sortable
- CSV import from bank statements

### 3. **Budget vs. Actuals — Stay In Control**
- Set monthly spend limits by category
- View actual vs. budgeted spend
- Traffic-light status indicators
- Drill down to category breakdowns
- Annual view for full-year analysis

### 4. **Insights & Analytics — Understand Your Money**
- Cash Flow Chart (6-month trends)
- Net Worth Over Time (monthly snapshots)
- Category Breakdown visualisations
- Month-vs-Month Comparison
- Sankey Diagram for income allocation

### 5. **Bills & Recurring Expenses**
- Log one-time and recurring bills
- Mark paid to clear alerts
- Auto-detect recurring transactions
- Frequency setting for accurate totals

### 6. **Superannuation Tracking & Projection**
- Track current balance for each account
- Input SGC rate and extra contributions
- Model 4 growth scenarios
- Projection to retirement age
- Inflation adjustment calculations

### 7. **Mortgage & Home Equity**
- Record home value and mortgage balance
- Link offset account to net exposure
- Track equity growth
- Offset impact calculator

### 8. **Liabilities — Complete Debt Picture**
- Record all debts (mortgages, car loans, credit cards, personal loans)
- Track interest rates and payment progress
- Total liability view
- Debt ratio as % of gross assets

### 9. **Savings Goals — Visual Progress**
- Create goals with target amount and date
- Link to Cash Tracker account for live balance
- Auto-calculate time to goal
- Emoji icons for personalisation

### 10. **Insurance & Life Policies**
- Log all insurance policies
- Track premiums and renewal dates
- Life insurance needs calculator

### 11. **Equity Holdings — Investment Portfolio**
- Record shares, ETFs, crypto, RSUs, options
- Track cost base and performance
- Calculate gain/loss and return %
- Portfolio allocation view

### 12. **Cash Tracker — Multi-Account Balance Log**
- Record account balances across multiple accounts
- Monthly snapshots for comparison
- Feed net worth calculations

### 13. **Multi-Profile & Family Sharing**
- Two profiles with PIN protection
- Shared household data
- Personal transaction tracking
- "Joint" view for household totals

### 14. **Quick Start Guide**
- 7-step onboarding walkthrough
- Auto-completion as actions are taken
- Progress tracking with celebration

## Notes for the Website Builder

1. **Simplicity First**: The app speaks for itself. Let the UI and real screenshots do the heavy lifting.
2. **Privacy Messaging**: This is the #1 differentiator. Lead with it.
3. **Australian Focus**: Use Australian examples (Commonwealth Bank, Westpac, SMSF, offset accounts, etc.).
4. **Mobile-First**: 50%+ of visitors will be on mobile. Make sure CTA is always visible and tappable.
5. **Accessibility**: Kelda's target users include older Australians (45–65). Ensure good contrast, readable fonts, clear navigation.
6. **No Fluff**: Avoid unnecessary animations, autoplay videos, or intrusive popups. Australians value straightforwardness.
7. **Trust Signals**: Include security/privacy badges, testimonials (with permission), or GitHub stars if open-sourced.

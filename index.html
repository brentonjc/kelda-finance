How to use Cash Tracker
Manual monthly balance snapshots — tap to expand
▼
📅
When to update
Record your balance whenever you check your accounts — you don't need to wait until month-end. Each entry is stored against a month (e.g. May 2025) but the date updated field lets you record exactly when you logged it.
💡
What balance to enter
Enter the closing balance — whatever the account shows when you log in. An entry mid-month is better than no entry. You can edit it later if you check again at month-end.
📊
Why it matters
Monthly snapshots power your Net Worth trend, Health Score calculations, and year-on-year comparisons on the Dashboard. Each account's history is shown as a trend line on the chart below.
🔧

## 3. Problem statement

**Section heading:** Your money is scattered. Kelda pulls it together.

Most people manage their finances across a banking app, a super portal, a mortgage login, a spreadsheet and a lot of guesswork. Nothing talks to each other, and the tools that *do* promise a full picture want to plug into your bank and hold your data on their servers.

Kelda takes a different path: **one app, all your finances, all on your device.** You stay in control of what goes in — and nobody else ever sees it.

---

## 4. How it works (3 steps)

1. **Open Kelda** — Launch it in any browser or add it to your home screen. No account, no download, no setup wizard to sit through.
2. **Add your finances** — Import bank transactions by CSV, or add them manually. Log your accounts, super, mortgage, goals and assets in minutes. The **Quick Start** guide walks you through it.
3. **See everything clearly** — Your dashboard, net worth, budgets, insights and forecasts update instantly — and it all stays on your device.

---

## 5. Why Kelda (differentiators)

- **Truly private.** All data lives in your browser. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. We literally can't see your finances.
- **Built for Australia.** AUD throughout, plus offset accounts, superannuation projections, mortgage/equity tracking and household conventions — designed for how Australians actually manage money.
- **Works everywhere, even offline.** Once loaded, Kelda runs with no internet. Add transactions and review insights on a plane or in a paddock.
- **Installs like an app.** Add to home screen and Kelda launches full-screen like a native iOS or Android app.
- **Made for households.** Separate PIN-protected profiles, a shared household view, and per-person transaction tracking.
- **Free, forever.** No subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium tier. Nothing to upsell because there's no server to pay for.

## 6. Feature showcase

Grouped the way the app itself is organised. Each block = one feature card (title, one-line hook, supporting detail).

### Dashboard — your financial picture at a glance
See net worth with a historical trend, a 0–100 health score, income/expense/savings KPIs, savings and budget habit streaks, and smart insights that nudge you in the right direction. Rearrange it your way with the dashboard layout switcher.

### Transactions — every dollar, categorised
Add, edit and delete transactions with merchant names, tag them to a person, bulk-recategorise in one go, and search or sort the full history. Handles large ledgers smoothly.

### Bank Accounts — balances over time
Track balances across all your accounts with monthly snapshots that feed straight into your net worth.

### Budget — stay in control
Set monthly limits by category and see actual vs. budgeted spend with traffic-light status. Drill into any category, or switch to an annual view for the full-year picture.

### Goals — visual savings progress
Create goals with a target amount and date, link them to a real account for a live balance, and let Kelda calculate your time-to-goal. Add an emoji to make each one yours.

### Bills — never miss a due date
Log one-time and recurring bills, mark them paid to clear alerts, and let Kelda auto-detect recurring payments and their frequency.

### Graphs & Insights — understand your money
Cash-flow trends over six months, net worth over time, category breakdowns, month-vs-month comparisons, a Sankey view of where your income goes, plus dedicated income and expense insight views — and a cash-flow **Forecast** that projects the months ahead.

### Mortgage & Home Equity
Record home value and loan balance, link an offset account to see your net exposure, track equity growth and model the impact of your offset.

### Liabilities — the full debt picture
Capture every debt — mortgage, car loan, credit card, personal loan — with interest rates and payment progress, a total-liability view, and your debt as a percentage of gross assets.

### Superannuation — track and project
Track each super balance, set your contribution rate and extra contributions, model growth scenarios and project your balance to retirement, adjusted for inflation.

### Investments (Equity Holdings)
Record shares, ETFs, crypto, RSUs and options with cost base, gain/loss, return %, and a portfolio allocation view.

### Insurance
Log every policy with premiums and renewal dates, and size up your cover with a life-insurance needs calculator.

### Smart Rules & Categories
Set a rule once — "contains Woolies" → Groceries — and watch new transactions sort themselves. 16+ categories, fully editable, with custom categories and subcategories.

### Import & Export
Bring in bank statements by CSV with auto-categorisation, and export everything to CSV or JSON any time for a full backup you own.

### Multi-profile & household sharing
Two PIN-protected profiles, shared household data, per-person tracking and a combined "Joint" view.

### Quick Start
A guided onboarding checklist that ticks itself off as you go, so you're up and running fast.

## 9. FAQ

**Is Kelda really free?**
Yes — free forever. There's no subscription, no premium tier and nothing to buy. Because there's no server behind it, there's nothing to charge for.

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely in your own browser on your device. Kelda has no servers and no accounts, so your financial data never leaves your device.

**Does Kelda connect to my bank?**
No — and that's the point. You bring data in via CSV import or manual entry, so your bank credentials are never involved.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Once loaded, Kelda works with no internet connection. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like a native app.

**Can my partner and I both use it?**
Yes. Kelda supports separate PIN-protected profiles with shared household data and a combined view.

**What happens if I clear my browser or switch devices?**
Because data is local, export a CSV/JSON backup regularly and re-import it on the new device or after clearing storage.

**Is it built for Australia?**
Completely — AUD, superannuation, offset accounts, mortgage/equity tracking and Australian household conventions throughout.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It runs in any modern browser. Installing to your home screen is optional and just gives you the full-screen app experience.

## 13. Notes for whoever builds the site

- **Lead with privacy.** It's the single biggest differentiator — put it above the fold and repeat it.
- **Use real screenshots.** The UI is the strongest asset; let it carry the feature sections.
- **Australian examples.** Reference offset accounts, super/SMSF, real bank names, council rates.
- **Mobile-first.** Most visitors arrive on a phone — keep the CTA always visible and tappable (44px min targets).
- **Accessible.** Some users are 45–65; ensure strong contrast, readable sizes and clear navigation.
- **No fluff.** Skip the marketing theatre. Clarity converts here.
